FitFlow 7 and Health Connect

When you finish a workout in the FitFlow 7 Android app, it writes one record to Health Connect: the active calories burned over that workout's time window, estimated from its length.

FitFlow 7 reads nothing from Health Connect, and nothing it writes there leaves your phone through FitFlow 7.

You can remove FitFlow 7's access, or delete what it wrote, at any time in Health Connect's settings.

The app itself works without an account; if you sign in to sync your history, that history is stored with FitFlow 7's own service, separately from Health Connect.
